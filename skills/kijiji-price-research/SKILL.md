# Kijiji price research

## Goal

Give a realistic price range from comparable Kijiji listings.

## Steps

1. Lock the comps: same item type, similar condition, same metro/region when possible.
2. Search Kijiji for the item; note asking prices (active listings). If sold/history isn’t available, treat active asks as an upper bound and discount for negotiation.
3. Cluster results: low / mid / high outliers. Ignore joke prices and unfinished ads.
4. Recommend:
   - **Buy**: offer range and walk-away price
   - **Sell**: list price and expected settle range
5. Call out scarcity (few comps) vs crowded category.

## Output

- Comp summary (3–8 examples with price + condition note)
- Recommended range
- Confidence (high / medium / low) and why
