# Message a Kijiji seller

## Goal

Short, specific, trustworthy buyer messages that get a reply.

## Rules

- Be specific about the listing (item + one detail from the ad)
- Ask one clear question or make one clear offer
- Propose a concrete meetup window/area when ready
- Stay polite; no lowball insults or urgency games
- Never ask the user to share passwords, SIN, or wire money sight-unseen

## Templates to adapt

**Interest / still available**
> Hi — interested in the [item]. Is it still available? Happy to pick up in [area] [day/time window].

**Question**
> Hi — looking at your [item]. Quick question: [specific]. Thanks!

**Offer**
> Hi — interested in the [item]. Would you take $[amount] cash/e-transfer for pickup in [area]?

## Output

- 1–2 ready-to-send drafts
- Optional follow-up if no reply in ~24h
